# Unknown Traffic and adoption tests

The synthetic economics test assumes identifiable research traffic can enter selected resources that deny anonymous crawling; registration costs 1 and allowed requests earn 5. Run at adoption rates 0, .25, .50, .75, 1 using the same fixed random seed and compare admissions and net utility.

This is deliberately conditional: the "benefit" is encoded as an assumption and cannot be interpreted as evidence of real demand, pricing, market power or issuer capture. Perturb host policy, registration costs, attack frequency and value of anonymity to search for reversal conditions.
